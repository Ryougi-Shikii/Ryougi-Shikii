### 🔬 Research Spotlight: RITformer
**Retinex Illumination Transformer for Low-Light Enhancement**
*Proposed a novel architecture fusing Retinex decomposition with **Illumination-Guided Attention (IG-MSA)**.*

- 📊 **Results:** Achieved **~23.6 dB PSNR** and **~0.872 SSIM** on the LOL benchmark, outperforming classical and Transformer-based baselines.
- 💡 **Key Innovation:** Introduced a learnable spatially-adaptive factor $\alpha$ for per-region enhancement control.
